## Promotion Decision

Aufgabe 1 was promoted as the first reviewed non-Mikro1 `official-task-source` family for Makro1. Pass 2 promotes the remaining reviewed tasks from the same clean Makro-I exam.

Promoted families:

- `makro1.official-task.klausur-2022-nachtermin-a1-short-answer-core`

The task is a short-answer family covering real and nominal GDP, war effects on GDP, GDP/GNI assignment, disinflation versus deflation, marginal propensity and the ZZ curve, IS-LM with interest-rate control, central-bank balance sheet operations, the 2008/09 financial crisis, risk premia in the extended IS-LM model, and adaptive inflation expectations.

Aufgabe 2 covers a goods-market multiplier problem with central-bank-set interest rate, fixed tax, proportional income tax, autonomous consumption shock, an IS-LM sketch, and an intuitive fixed-tax versus income-tax comparison.

Aufgabe 3 covers wage-setting and price-setting equations, natural unemployment, real wage, a markup shock, and a suitable labor-market graph.

Aufgabe 4 covers an IS-LM-PC energy-price shock where natural output falls, plus a central-bank policy discussion. The visible official task numbering jumps from part `a)` to part `c)`; the portal metadata preserves this rather than normalizing it.

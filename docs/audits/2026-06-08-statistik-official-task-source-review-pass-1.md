## Task Coverage

Aufgabe 1 covers the scale level of a binary correctness variable and descriptive location/spread parameters for a sorted 0/1 series. The official prompt asks students to state the scale level, calculate or justify the non-applicability of mode, median, arithmetic mean, and empirical variance.

Aufgabe 2 covers descriptive analysis of beer-consumption data with a categorical origin variable. The official prompt asks for mean and empirical standard deviation, a suitable location measure for a nominal variable, discrete/continuous and scale-level classification for both variables, an empirical distribution function, and the mean for grouped data.
